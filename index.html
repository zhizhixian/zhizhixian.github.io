Hi! I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Statistics at Rutgers University, advised by Prof. Linjun Zhang. Before Rutgers, I received my B.S. in Statistics from Zhejiang University in 2025.

My research focuses on the statistical foundations of AI. I am interested in statistically grounded evaluation of large language models, LLM post-training, semiparametric inference and machine learning theory.
